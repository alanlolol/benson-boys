# Benson Boys

A cozy 8-bit life game set in Bensonhurst, Brooklyn. You play as Alex, one of the four Benson Boys, starting in high school and living at home.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser, or play the hosted version once GitHub Pages is on.

- **Computer.** Arrow keys or WASD to move, E or Space to talk or use, Q for the journal.
- **Phone.** On-screen pad to move, A to talk or use, B for the journal.

The game saves each morning in the browser you play in.

## What's in Chapter 1 so far

- One block near 18th Avenue and 86th Street, with the D train running overhead
- Alex's house, Valentino's Pizzeria (run by John), Mrs. Russo next door, a sneaker shop, and the high school with its football field
- Energy that drains with tasks and refills with sleep, and sleep quality that drops if you stay up late, skip dinner, or game past 10
- A curfew, with grounding when you miss it
- Odd jobs for money, and upgrades that make life easier (sneakers for speed, a tutor for cheaper homework)
- Friendships that unlock invitations you can accept or decline
